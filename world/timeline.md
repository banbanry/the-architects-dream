# Timeline

## Story Timeline (In-Universe)

### Before the Story
- **T-10 years**: The architect begins his career. Designs the PEF framework over several years.
- **T-?**: The system begins fading. UI borders dissolve, inventory numbers drift, log hashes change meaning, microservice boundaries melt. The cause is unknown.
- **T-?**: Seven previous architects attempted to fix the fade. All failed. Their frameworks became part of the fade.

### Prologue: The Night of the Incident
- **02:00 AM**: Production incident. P99 latency spikes, connection pool exhausted, circuit breakers tripping, retry storms cascading.
- **02:15 AM**: The architect identifies the root cause — not a code bug, but the system itself fading.
- **02:30 AM**: The architect activates the PEF calibration device. Five-domain isolation engages. StateLedger begins recording.
- **02:45 AM**: The first hammer begins to descend. Descartes' Ghost appears from code comments.
- **StateLedger timestamp**: `02:45:12` → shifts to `02:45:13` after Hammer 1.

### Chapter 1: Descartes' Ghost (Hammer 1 — Subject Layer)
- Descartes emerges from code comments. Comments modify themselves: "indestructible" → "inferred."
- Four strikes: (1) cogito proves thinking exists, not "I"; (2) audit doesn't require subject (blockchain, formal verification, differential privacy counterexamples); (3) P's accountability overlaps with existing tools (git blame, CI/CD, OpenTelemetry); (4) P's boundary undefined in multi-agent systems.
- **Correction**: P repositioned as "useful engineering convention," not metaphysical necessity.
- **StateLedger page 1** written.
- **Dashboard**: "Completeness check: FAILED" appears.
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 2: Gödel's Loop (Hammer 2 — Logic Layer)
- Gödel emerges from the dissolving definition of P.
- Three strikes: (1) emergent behavior (microservice cascade failure positive feedback loop, no single subject); (2) game behavior (two AI agents conversing, result is fixed point of two functions); (3) creative behavior (AI code generator invents new design pattern, E is dynamically self-updating).
- **Correction**: P/E/F repositioned as "useful decomposition within declared scope," not universal paradigm. Internal contradiction resolved (P as convention vs. indestructible).
- **StateLedger page 2** written.
- **Dashboard**: "Verifiability check: FAILED" appears.
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 3: Nietzsche's Hammer (Hammer 3 — Verifiability Layer)
- Nietzsche smashes out of the screen. Monitoring screen shatters into fragments, each showing a past claim.
- Three strikes: (1) demands others verify but doesn't verify itself (demo's 8/8 PASS verifies demo runs, not framework effectiveness); (2) "post-hoc explanation tool" is an unfalsifiable protective shell (but architect uses PEF for decisions daily); (3) four functions (audit/trace/verify/reproduce) all insufficiently empirically validated.
- **Correction**: PEF repositioned as "decision-assistance engine." "Verifiability Declaration" written.
- **StateLedger page 3** written.
- **Dashboard**: "Choice layer check: FAILED" appears.
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 4: Turing's Crush (Hammer 4 — Choice Layer)
- Turing walks out of an infinite tape of 0s and 1s.
- Four strikes: (1) you make choices every second (whether to answer, tone, length); (2) choice constraints (E_out) and consequences (F) are within the framework; (3) does the instant of choice really exist? (function? random? free will? oracle?); (4) every step of 1→N contains micro-choices, 0→1 and 1→N are intertwined.
- **Correction**: Choice redefined as three-layer structure (constraints describable, consequences describable, instant of choice indescribable). Framework assists decisions, doesn't make them.
- **Turing's revelation**: A tape of π's binary expansion. The read/write head hesitates once every 10,000 steps. That 0.01% is the instant of choice. That 0.01% is free will.
- **StateLedger page 4** written.
- **Dashboard**: "Physics layer check: FAILED" appears.
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 5: Schrödinger's Cat (Hammer 5 — Physics Layer)
- Schrödinger emerges from a closed black metal box. Cat meowing from inside. Cat is both alive and dead.
- Four strikes: (1) no clear boundary between macroscopic and quantum (quantum computers, superconducting circuits, photosynthesis); (2) "the real cat isn't like that" changes the question; (3) "dissipative system" definition inaccurate (frictionless pendulum, planetary orbits are conservative systems but PEF can describe them); (4) PEF's understanding of physics is qualitative metaphor, not quantitative modeling.
- **Correction**: PEF applies to "systems with traceable causal chains," not limited to macroscopic or dissipative. Physical concept usage marked as qualitative metaphor.
- **Schrödinger's closing line**: "You can never know the result without changing the result."
- **StateLedger page 5** written.
- **Dashboard**: "Time layer check: FAILED" appears.
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 6: Einstein's Verdict (Hammer 6 — Time Layer)
- Einstein emerges from the bending of spacetime. Monitoring screen bends like gravitational lensing.
- Four strikes: (1) π-anchor assumes Newtonian absolute time, but real time is relative (even in distributed systems); (2) π's five-property combination is irreplaceable (no alternative has all five); (3) three core mechanisms depend on π (π-Mod3 phase allocation, content-bound π scheduling, π-anchor coordinates); (4) anti-vector collapse is unverified.
- **Correction**: π repositioned as core component of engineering implementation layer. Anti-vector collapse marked as hypothetical function pending verification. HLC + π-anchor recommended for distributed environments.
- **The black hole (anti-vector collapse) emits Hawking radiation** — a hypothesis to be verified is not a false concept.
- **StateLedger page 6** written.
- **Dashboard**: "Space layer check: FAILED" appears.
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 7: Kant's Space (Hammer 7 — Space Layer)
- Kant emerges from the unfolding of infinite space. Screen boundaries dissolve, content extends infinitely.
- Four strikes: (1) mod-defined space is the subject's behavior, not the object's property; (2) mod is the simplest of many space division methods (interval, hash sharding, consistent hashing, range sharding, R-tree, Voronoi, B-tree); (3) equivalence relations are not mod's patent; (4) using Kant's spatial philosophy to endorse mod is far-fetched (Kant's space is a priori, mod is empirical and optional).
- **Correction**: mod repositioned as simple, uniform, stateless space division method. Philosophical endorsement removed.
- **StateLedger page 7** written.
- **Dashboard**: "Core layer check: FAILED" appears.
- **π's voice**: "Seven hammers are over. Now, it's my turn."
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 8: The Position of π (Hammer 8 — Core Layer, π Self-Shattering)
- π emerges from a waterfall of numbers. Infinite, non-repeating digits cascade like a galaxy.
- π shatters itself. Seven-step argument:
  1. π is not E (given, a priori, cannot be modified)
  2. π is not P (doesn't do anything, just exists; the architect uses π, subject-object reversal)
  3. π is not F (exists prior to all subjects/variables/moments)
  4. π is coordinate/reference frame/measurement benchmark (π is what gives all other variables position)
  5. π's position is undeterminable (π is the benchmark for determining position; cannot use the benchmark to determine the benchmark's own position)
  6. π is the framework's homunculus (core but not inside the framework, position undeterminable, infinite regress of foundations)
  7. π is a foundation assumption (PEF assumes π exists, infinite non-repeating, reproducible, globally consistent; these assumptions are unprovable — Gödel's incompleteness theorem)
- **Correction**: π repositioned as foundation assumption, framework's homunculus, unprovable, vulnerable, framework root with undeterminable position.
- **StateLedger pages 8-15** written (π's self-shattering spans multiple pages).
- **Dashboard**: "Shadow layer check: FAILED" appears.
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second.

### Chapter 9: The Shadow of the Cave (Hammer 9 — Shadow Layer, Shadow Self-Shattering)
- The Shadow emerges from a two-dimensional plane. Every character casts a flat, thicknessless shadow.
- The Shadow shatters the final illusion. Seven-part argument:
  1. P/E/F is a coordinate system — reality projected into it loses depth (emotion, consciousness, free will, meaning, value)
  2. Two-dimensional perfect circle vs. physical pixel circle — PEF is the perfect circle (perfect but depthless), reality is the pixel circle (imperfect but deep)
  3. Plato's Cave — PEF is the cave, P/E/F is the wall, the seven philosophers are those who escaped
  4. Value of shadows — an honest shadow can truly reflect reality; a dishonest shadow distorts and fades
  5. Root cause of system fading — not vector collapse, not memory pollution, but the dishonesty of the shadow
  6. From Laplace's Demon to honest observer — not downgrading, upgrading
  7. True function of calibration device — not to eliminate illusions, but to make shadows honest
- **Correction**: PEF repositioned as "honest shadow of reality."
- **StateLedger page 16** written. "Shadow of the Cave Declaration."
- **Dashboard**: Eight "FAILED" lines converge into one golden line: **"Honesty check: PASSED."**
- **Timestamp shift**: +1 second. System fade completely stops.

### Epilogue: The Calibration Device in the Ashes
- The system stabilizes. Not perfect stability — honest stability.
- StateLedger: 16 pages. Nine hammers. Nine layers of illusion. All shattered.
- The calibration device continues to run. Not to eliminate illusions. To make shadows honest.
- Ashes preserved in StateLedger appendix, review-response.md, version-history.md, git history.
- A new day begins. A new audit task comes in. The architect starts the calibration device. But this time, he knows these are just shadows. He casts them honestly.
- **Final line**: "An honest shadow doesn't fade."

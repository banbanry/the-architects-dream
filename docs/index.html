// ABOUT THE STORY

What is this about?

A journey from illusion to honesty, told through nine philosophical hammers.

An architect believes he is Laplace's Demon. He has mastered the P/E/F framework — Subject, Variable, Result. With enough variables, he can predict anything. With the right combination, he can win everything.

Then the system begins to fade. Colors wash out. Integers lose their edges. Seven philosophers arrive — Descartes, Gödel, Nietzsche, Turing, Schrödinger, Einstein, Kant — each with a hammer. Each shatters one layer of his illusion.

After the seventh hammer, he chooses to live. Not as a demon, but as a teacher. Then π reveals itself — the one thing no hammer can shatter. The foundation assumption beneath everything. The homunculus at the core of the framework.

The ninth hammer is the shadow itself. The story is a shadow of reality. The framework is a shadow of the world. And honesty is not about eliminating shadows — it's about measuring how far the shadow is from the thing itself.

// PEF FIRST PRINCIPLES
P

Subject (主体)

Who is doing. Must have a name, boundary, unit.

E

Variable (变量)

What is used. E_in = controllable, E_out = uncontrollable.

F

Result (结果)

What is obtained. Must be traceable to (P, E, t).

π ANCHOR

The undeterminable foundation. Outside P/E/F. The reference frame that gives all variables their position. Unprovable. Vulnerable. Essential.
